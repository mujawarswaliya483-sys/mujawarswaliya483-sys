## A little about me

Hey, I'm **Swaliya** — a second-year Computer Science Engineering student in India.

I like taking an idea and figuring out how to turn it into something that actually works. Sometimes that means building a user interface; sometimes it means understanding a backend problem, fixing a bug, or going back to learn a concept I thought I understood.

I'm working towards becoming a stronger full-stack developer. I care about understanding the code I write, not just getting a project to run once.

At the moment, most of my learning happens through projects, experiments, and the occasional stubborn bug.

### 01 / LUMI

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/STATUS-IN%20PROGRESS-263429?style=flat-square&labelColor=263429&color=B6CF91" alt="In progress"/>

**A more thoughtful wellbeing experience.**

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/PROJECT-HACKATHON%20PROTOTYPE-263429?style=flat-square&labelColor=263429&color=B6CF91" alt="Hackathon prototype"/>

I want to create an interactive, sketch-style digital guide to my college, with spaces people can explore and a way to find classrooms, labs, and faculty cabins.
